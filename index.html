    Where Campus Emissions Come From

    🚗 Daily Commuting & Transport

    Students and staff travelling by cars and motorbikes generate emissions. Shared transport and cycling can reduce the environmental impact.

    ⚡ Classroom & Hostel Energy

    Lights, fans, air conditioners and computers consume electricity. Energy-efficient appliances and responsible usage can reduce emissions.

    ♻️ Canteen Food Waste & Packaging

    Food waste and single-use packaging contribute to environmental pollution and greenhouse gas emissions.

    📄 Paper & Printing Lifecycle

    Paper production and printing require trees, water and energy. Digital submissions and double-sided printing help conserve resources.

    🌿 Steps to Reduce Carbon Emissions

    • Use Sustainable Transport: Walk, cycle, carpool or use public transport whenever possible.
    • Reduce Electricity Consumption: Switch off lights, fans and ACs when they are not needed.
    • Choose Reusable Products: Carry reusable bottles, cups and bags instead of disposable ones.
    • Reduce Food Waste: Take only the food you need and support composting initiatives.
    • Save Water: Close taps, repair leaks and encourage rainwater harvesting.
    • Go Paperless: Use digital assignments, notices and learning materials.
    • Plant and Protect Trees: Participate in campus plantation and biodiversity programmes.
    • Use Renewable Energy: Encourage solar panels and energy-efficient campus buildings.

    🌍 Climate Change & Global Warming

    Understand climate change and its environmental effects.

    Explore topic +

    Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperature and weather patterns. Human activities such as fossil fuel combustion, deforestation and industrial processes increase greenhouse gas concentrations.

    Protective steps:
    • Reduce fossil fuel consumption.
    • Promote clean energy.
    • Protect forests.
    • Reduce waste and pollution.

    ☀️ Renewable Energy

    Discover sustainable sources of energy.

    Explore topic +

    Renewable energy comes from naturally replenished sources such as sunlight, wind and flowing water.

    Major types:
    • Solar energy.
    • Wind energy.
    • Hydroelectric energy.
    • Geothermal energy.
    • Biomass energy.

    ♻️ Waste Management

    Learn responsible waste management methods.

    Explore topic +

    Waste management involves collecting, separating, treating and disposing of waste responsibly.

    Five Rs:
    • Refuse unnecessary products.
    • Reduce consumption.
    • Reuse materials.
    • Repurpose old products.
    • Recycle suitable materials.

    🌳 Biodiversity & Ecosystems

    Understand the importance of protecting living organisms.

    Explore topic +

    Biodiversity includes the variety of plants, animals and microorganisms. Healthy ecosystems support food chains, pollination, soil fertility and clean water.

    Protection methods:
    • Protect natural habitats.
    • Prevent deforestation.
    • Reduce pollution.
    • Support wildlife conservation.

    💧 Water Conservation

    Learn how to protect freshwater resources.

    Explore topic +

    Water conservation means using water efficiently and protecting freshwater resources.

    Methods:
    • Rainwater harvesting.
    • Repair leaking taps.
    • Reuse treated wastewater.
    • Protect rivers and lakes.

    🌱 Sustainable Development

    Learn how development can protect future generations.

    Explore topic +

    Sustainable development meets present needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

    Three main pillars:
    • Environmental sustainability.
    • Social sustainability.
    • Economic sustainability.

    About Campus EcoTrack

    Campus EcoTrack is an Environmental Science project focused on estimating carbon footprints, encouraging sustainable behaviour and supporting environmental awareness across university campuses.

    Our objective is to encourage students, faculty and institutions to take meaningful action towards environmental protection and sustainable development.
