Where Campus Emissions Come From

Daily Commuting & Transport

Students and staff travelling by car and motorbike burn fuel every day. Shared and electric options cut emissions per person.

Classroom & Hostel Energy

Lights, fans, air conditioners and computers use electricity, which often comes from fossil fuels.

Canteen Food Waste & Packaging

Wasted food rots in landfills and releases methane, while single-use packaging adds plastic waste.

Paper & Printing Lifecycle

Making, printing and discarding paper uses trees, water and energy. Digital submission reduces all three.

Recommendations for University Administration

  • Dedicated shuttle services: run electric or CNG shuttles between hostels, bus stops and academic blocks to reduce private vehicle use.
  • Paperless submission rules: accept assignments, forms and notices online, and make double-sided printing the default.
  • Cafeteria composting: turn food scraps into compost for campus gardens and replace disposable packaging with reusable plates.
  • Solar energy: install rooftop solar panels on hostels and academic buildings to lower grid electricity demand.
